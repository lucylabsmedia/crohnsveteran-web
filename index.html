A Crohn's veteran in every sense

Jordan B. McConnell was 14 when emergency surgery revealed Crohn's disease. He went on to serve in the U.S. Air Force, until Crohn's returned and ended the military career he loved. What he did next became Crohn's Veteran.

When Jordan looked for men, especially men of color, talking openly about IBD, he found almost no one. So he started sharing his own life online, then built a podcast and a community so others would feel less alone.

By day, Jordan is a cloud engineer at New American Funding. He brings that experience back to the community, building tools and products for people with IBD and everyone who supports them.

“Building systems that give people clarity, independence, and pride—no matter what health battle they are fighting.”

The journey

From a diagnosis at 14 to a community built for others.

  1. Age 14

    Diagnosed in ninth grade

    An emergency surgery ends with a Crohn's diagnosis.

  2. USAF

    Serving in the Air Force

    Rises from Airman Basic to Staff Sergeant. At Langley Air Force Base, he helps run network operations supporting about 100,000 people across 15 bases.

  3. Guard

    Air National Guard

    Keeps serving in California and Nebraska while working in civilian tech support.

  4. 2009

    Crohn's returns

    Hospital stays and another major surgery follow. Ruled non-deployable, he receives an honorable discharge.

  5. Rebuild

    Starting over in tech

    Earns a bachelor's degree and works his way from tech support to network administration to cloud engineering.

  6. 2020

    The podcast launches

    After a year of sharing his story on Instagram, Jordan starts the Crohn's Veteran Podcast with co-hosts CJ “Papuro” Cabrera and Renika Wood.

  7. 2023

    75 episodes later

    The podcast signs off on World IBD Day with its final episode.

  8. Today

    Building for the community

    A cloud engineer at New American Funding, Jordan builds tools and products for the IBD community and the people who care about them.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to have IBD to work with Crohn's Veteran?

No. We build tools and products for the IBD community and for the people who care about them, including caregivers, families, and allies whose tech needs have nothing to do with IBD.

Is the podcast still releasing new episodes?

The Crohn's Veteran Podcast released its 75th and final episode on World IBD Day 2023. The full archive is free on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and YouTube.

How do I reach Jordan about a tool, website, or app?

Use the form below and choose the closest inquiry type, or email jordan@crohnsveteran.com. Share a short overview of your idea and timeline. Jordan reviews every technical scope himself and follows up within 24–48 hours.

How does purchasing and checkout work for awareness gear?

Clicking “Buy now” on any item opens our hosted Printful Quick Store (crohnsveteranstore.printful.me), where you can pick your size, select color variations, and check out securely via Stripe with global door-to-door tracking.

Can Jordan speak on podcasts, webinars, or keynotes?

Yes. Jordan speaks on living and working with Crohn's, military and career transitions, fatherhood with IBD, and why the patient community needs more men's voices.

Is Crohn's Veteran only for military veterans?

Not at all. While founded by an Air Force veteran, Crohn's Veteran is for anyone navigating Crohn's, colitis, or autoimmune GI disorders worldwide, and for the people who support them.
