The Story Behind The Mission

Turning Chronic Challenge into Collective Strength

After serving in the United States Air Force and navigating a complex, lifelong journey with Crohn’s disease, Jordan founded Crohn’s Veteran to ensure no patient, caregiver, or advocate feels isolated in their diagnosis.

Combining disciplined problem-solving with transparent storytelling, Jordan has hosted over 75 podcast episodes, engaged with national healthcare forums, and spotlighted patient journeys from all walks of life. His work focuses on dismantling medical biases, sharing gut-friendly culinary insights, and raising critical awareness for Inflammatory Bowel Disease worldwide.
